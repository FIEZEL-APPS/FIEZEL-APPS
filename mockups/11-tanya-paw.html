Hai Rani. Lagi di lesson Present Simple, ya. Ada yang bikin bingung?

Gampang kok. Lihat subjeknya dulu:
I / you / we / they → don't
They don't eat meat.
he / she / it → doesn't
She doesn't eat meat.
Ingat: setelah doesn't, kata kerjanya polos, tanpa -s.
