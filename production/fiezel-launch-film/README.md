# FIEZEL Launch Film — THE WAY YOU LEARN

Production package for the 40–43s vertical FIEZEL launch film.

## Status

This branch contains a **production animatic / motion master**, not a claim of finished CGI. The connected video-generation workspace did not expose a video model during production, so the film was built as a deterministic motion-graphics animatic with procedural sound design.

The animatic is designed to lock story, timing, camera rhythm, PAW beats, localization beats, CTA, subtitle timing, and social cutdown structure before final compositing.

## Creative lock

- Campaign: **THE WAY YOU LEARN**
- Primary line: **LEARN ENGLISH. YOUR WAY.**
- Expansion line: **Now, FIEZEL speaks Thai.**
- Master format: 9:16 vertical, 1080×1920, 43 seconds
- Master VO strategy: English VO only
- Localized subtitle masters: Indonesian and Thai
- Product is the hero; PAW is the emotional bridge; learning intelligence is the story; Thai is the expansion.

## PAW identity

PAW is anchored to the repository’s canonical animation-ready master at:

`design/paw-redesign/assets/paw-master.svg`

Do not replace PAW with a robot or redesign the silhouette. Final compositing should use the canonical SVG/production rig directly. The animatic redraw is only a timing proxy based on the canonical proportions and closed PAW palette.

## Truthfulness / UI rule

The animatic contains dimensional **proxy UI panels only for motion blocking and timing**. They must not be presented as product screenshots in the final advertisement.

For final production, replace every product-facing proxy panel with real FIEZEL captures for:

- learning activities
- vocabulary
- grammar
- listening
- speaking
- practice
- progress
- Indonesian localization
- Thai localization

Do not fabricate product functionality.
