What FIEZEL Reading Exercises Train

Reading comprehension in FIEZEL trains five core cognitive reading competencies:

1. Gist & Main Idea

Identifying the overarching theme, argument, or communicative purpose of a paragraph or complete passage.

2. Specific Detail Retrieval

Locating concrete facts, chronological sequences, data points, and direct assertions within text under timed conditions.

3. Vocabulary in Context

Deducing the precise meaning of unfamiliar words and idiomatic phrases from surrounding textual clues and register.

4. Inference & Deduction

Drawing valid logical conclusions about implied perspectives, unstated motivations, and subtext not explicitly spelled out.

5. Tone & Author Perspective

Recognizing irony, bias, rhetorical framing, academic objectivity, and emotional stance across advanced C1 and C2 texts.

CEFR Reading Level Progression

The reading bank is calibrated so learners develop stamina and fluency progressively:

  • A1 (Breakthrough): 50–100 words. Simple everyday topics: emails, schedules, invitations, personal introductions.
  • A2 (Waystage): 120–200 words. Biographies, travel descriptions, factual workplace memos, simple narratives.
  • B1 (Threshold): 250–350 words. News reports, cultural features, opinion pieces, practical procedural guides.
  • B2 (Vantage): 400–550 words. Analytical articles, technical overviews, social essays, complex character narratives.
  • C1 (Effective Operational): 600–800 words. Academic papers, legal/business commentary, literary prose with figurative language.
  • C2 (Mastery): 800+ words. Abstract philosophical arguments, satire, dense scholarly analysis, intricate prose style.
