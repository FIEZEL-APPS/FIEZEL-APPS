Matriks Referensi Lengkap: Rumus 12 Tenses Bahasa Inggris (3 Waktu × 4 Aspek)

Dalam Bahasa Indonesia, kata kerja tidak pernah berubah bentuk (kata "makan" tetap sama untuk kemarin, sekarang, maupun besok). Dalam Bahasa Inggris, setiap kata kerja wajib mencerminkan Waktu dan Aspek (Sifat Kejadian). Gunakan tabel di bawah ini sebagai peta induk:

Nama Tense & Level CEFR Rumus Inti (+) Fungsi Logis & Penanda Waktu Kesalahan Umum Penutur Indonesia (Salah vs Benar)
1. Simple Present
CEFR A1 · Unit 1–5		 S + V1 (s/es)
atau S + am/is/are		 Fakta umum, jadwal tetap, kebiasaan rutin (always, every day, usually). Salah: She work at a hospital.
Benar: She works at a hospital. (Lupa -s untuk subjek tunggal).
2. Present Continuous
CEFR A1 · Unit 6–9		 S + am/is/are + V-ing Sedang berlangsung tepat saat bicara atau proyek sementara (right now, currently). Salah: I studying right now.
Benar: I am studying right now. (Melewatkan to be am/is/are).
3. Present Perfect
CEFR A2–B1 · Unit 28–34		 S + have/has + V3 Dimulai di masa lalu dan masih berlanjut, atau baru selesai dan hasilnya terasa sekarang (since, for, already, yet). Salah: I live here since 2021.
Benar: I have lived here since 2021.
4. Present Perfect Continuous
CEFR B1 · Unit 35–38		 S + have/has + been + V-ing Menekankan durasi aktivitas yang terus berlangsung dari masa lalu hingga detik ini (for 3 hours, all morning). Salah: It is raining for two hours.
Benar: It has been raining for two hours.
5. Simple Past
CEFR A2 · Unit 18–23		 S + V2
Negatif: did not + V1		 Kejadian di titik waktu lampau yang sudah selesai sepenuhnya (yesterday, last night, in 2020, ... ago). Salah: I didn't went yesterday.
Benar: I didn't go yesterday. (Setelah did not wajib kembali ke V1).
6. Past Continuous
CEFR A2–B1 · Unit 24–27		 S + was/were + V-ing Kejadian yang sedang berlangsung di masa lalu ketika kejadian lain menyela (while, when). Salah: When you called, I slept.
Benar: When you called, I was sleeping.
7. Past Perfect
CEFR B1–B2 · Unit 55–59		 S + had + V3 "Masa lalunya masa lalu": kejadian yang sudah tuntas sebelum kejadian lampau lainnya terjadi (before, after, by the time). Salah: The train left before we arrived.
Benar: The train had left before we arrived.
8. Past Perfect Continuous
CEFR B2 · Unit 60–62		 S + had + been + V-ing Menekankan durasi suatu proses di masa lalu sebelum terhenti oleh peristiwa lampau lain. Salah: She was waiting for an hour when the bus finally came.
Benar: She had been waiting for an hour when the bus finally came.
9. Simple Future
CEFR A2 · Unit 29–32		 S + will + V1
atau be going to + V1		 Keputusan spontan/prediksi (will) atau rencana yang sudah diniatkan (be going to). Salah: I will to call you tomorrow.
Benar: I will call you tomorrow. (Setelah modal will tanpa to).
10. Future Continuous
CEFR B1–B2 · Unit 74–76		 S + will be + V-ing Kejadian yang akan sedang berlangsung pada jam/waktu spesifik di masa depan (at this time tomorrow). Salah: At 10 AM tomorrow, I will take the exam.
Benar: At 10 AM tomorrow, I will be taking the exam.
11. Future Perfect
CEFR B2 · Unit 77–80		 S + will have + V3 Kejadian yang dipastikan sudah selesai sebelum tenggat waktu tertentu di masa depan (by next month, by 2027). Salah: By next Friday, we will finish the project.
Benar: By next Friday, we will have finished the project.
12. Future Perfect Continuous
CEFR B2–C1 · Unit 81–83		 S + will have been + V-ing Akumulasi durasi yang akan telah berlangsung saat mencapai titik waktu di masa depan (by December, I will have been working here for 5 years). Salah: By May, she will work here for ten years.
Benar: By May, she will have been working here for ten years.
