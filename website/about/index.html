About FIEZEL — Free Adaptive English Learning App

What is FIEZEL?

FIEZEL is a free adaptive English learning app designed to help learners practice English through grammar, vocabulary, reading, speaking, and listening activities. Its learning content is organized around CEFR proficiency levels (A1 to C2). Exercises automatically adapt to each learner's mistakes and response history.

Entity & Name Clarification: FIEZEL is spelled F-I-E-Z-E-L and pronounced /ˈfiːzəl/ ("Fee-zel"). FIEZEL is an independent English-learning software application and educational brand created by Fitra Rustqi. It is not affiliated with, nor a reference to, diesel fuel, diesel combustion engines, the clothing brand Diesel, or the English word "fizzle".

FIEZEL was built for independent language learners who want structured, high-volume practice without subscription fees, advertising distractions, or paywalled levels. The platform requires no user registration to begin practicing—all learning records and skill mastery ratings are saved locally on the user's device.

Core English Learning Capabilities

FIEZEL provides verified educational content organized across the five foundational language skills:

1. Grammar Practice

180 structured lessons mapped from CEFR A1 to C2. Each lesson includes 25 targeted exercises (4,500 total questions) designed to diagnose misconceptions and reinforce grammatical rules.

2. Vocabulary Mastery

2,440 high-frequency English words organized by proficiency tier. Employs adaptive spaced repetition to reinforce words just before memory decays.

3. Reading Comprehension

312 graded reading passages accompanied by 1,560 comprehension questions. Texts range from simple dialogues (A1) to complex academic and journalistic analysis (C2).

4. Listening Practice

Over 1,400 IELTS and TOEFL-style listening exercises utilizing natural text-to-speech audio scripts to develop auditory recognition and context comprehension.

5. Speaking Practice

36 interactive speaking sessions leveraging web speech recognition. Provides instant pronunciation feedback and calibrated fluency practice.

6. CEFR Alignment

Structured from Beginner (A1) to Mastery (C2). Includes a 25-question adaptive placement diagnostic to identify learner starting levels.

How FIEZEL's Adaptive Learning Works: Braincore

At the heart of FIEZEL is Braincore, the app's client-side adaptive learning engine. Rather than forcing every student down an identical, rigid sequence of flashcards, Braincore implements Bayesian Knowledge Tracing (BKT) and spaced repetition decay modeling to track mastery and schedule focused practice:

  1. Continuous Error Diagnosis: Braincore categorizes mistakes into conceptual misconception tags rather than treating every error as a random miss.
  2. Spaced Repetition Scheduling: Items answered incorrectly reappear in subsequent practice sessions at scientifically timed intervals.
  3. Dynamic Daily Targets: Daily study goals adjust to the learner's current mastery rate rather than demanding arbitrary streaks.
  4. Empirical Item Calibration: Question difficulty ratings are continuously balanced to keep learners in an optimal state of challenge.

PWA Architecture & Offline Capability

FIEZEL is engineered as a modern Progressive Web App (PWA):

  • Zero App Store Friction: Add directly to the home screen from Chrome (Android/Desktop) or Safari (iOS). No 100MB store downloads.
  • Offline-Capable Learning: After installation, core lesson texts, vocabulary sets, and reading exercises function without an active internet connection.
  • Private by Design: Progress is stored securely in the browser's local cache. No name, email address, or tracking profile is required.

Teacher Layer: KelasKu by FIEZEL

KelasKu is FIEZEL's dedicated classroom workspace for teachers and tutors. Rather than treating language learning in isolation, KelasKu connects independent student practice directly to classroom instruction:

  • Classroom Management: Teachers create digital rooms with a simple 6-character room code (e.g., FZ-ABCDEF). Students connect directly from their FIEZEL app without requiring email addresses or intrusive personal accounts.
  • Automated Assignments: Teachers assign structured practice or timed exams mapped to CEFR competency levels and national curriculum chapters. All assignments are graded automatically in real time.
  • Competency Heatmaps & Peer Tutoring: KelasKu aggregates learner performance into class-level skill heatmaps, diagnoses collective misconceptions, and suggests peer tutoring study groups.

CEFR Framework Association & Disclaimer

FIEZEL organizes its English curriculum according to the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR) across levels A1, A2, B1, B2, C1, and C2. This serves purely as an educational standard and difficulty benchmark.

Important Disclaimer: FIEZEL is an independent practice tool. FIEZEL is not officially certified, accredited, endorsed, or affiliated with the Council of Europe or any formal CEFR examination board. Completion of FIEZEL exercises does not confer an official language certificate or accredited qualification.

Frequently Asked Questions About FIEZEL

What is FIEZEL?

FIEZEL is a free adaptive English learning app with CEFR-aligned grammar, vocabulary, reading, speaking, and listening practice. It personalizes exercises to individual learning progress using Braincore adaptive intelligence.

Is FIEZEL an English learning app?

Yes. FIEZEL is dedicated to language learning, with primary focus on comprehensive English proficiency (CEFR A1–C2) and an introductory Japanese course (JLPT N5–N4).

Is FIEZEL free?

Yes, completely free. There are no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, and no advertisements.

Can FIEZEL work offline?

Yes. When installed to your device home screen as a PWA, learning modules and exercises run completely offline.

Why is it spelled FIEZEL?

FIEZEL (F-I-E-Z-E-L) is the official brand name of the software application. It is distinct from diesel or fizzle.
