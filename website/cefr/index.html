FIEZEL CEFR Levels — A1 to C2 English Learning Framework

What is CEFR and how does FIEZEL use it?

The Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR) is an internationally recognized standard describing language ability across six proficiency tiers: A1, A2, B1, B2, C1, and C2. FIEZEL maps its 180 grammar lessons, 2,440 vocabulary items, 312 reading texts, speaking sessions, and listening banks to these CEFR levels so learners know precisely what difficulty to expect at each stage.

Important Non-Accreditation Disclaimer: CEFR is a language proficiency framework established by the Council of Europe; it is not a certification authority that licenses software. FIEZEL aligns its curriculum to CEFR difficulty levels for instructional benchmarking only. FIEZEL is not officially certified, accredited, or partnered with CEFR or Cambridge English, and completion of lessons does not grant an official certificate.

The Six CEFR Levels in FIEZEL

A1 · Breakthrough

Beginner Level (A1)

What the learner can do: Understand and use basic everyday expressions and very simple phrases aimed at satisfying concrete needs. Introduce themselves and others, ask and answer personal questions about where they live, people they know, and things they have.

FIEZEL A1 Content: 17 grammar lessons (425 exercises), basic high-frequency vocabulary, simple reading dialogues, and foundational acoustic listening exercises.

A2 · Waystage

Elementary Level (A2)

What the learner can do: Understand sentences and frequently used expressions related to areas of most immediate relevance (personal and family info, shopping, local geography, employment). Communicate in simple and routine tasks requiring a direct exchange of information.

FIEZEL A2 Content: 29 grammar lessons (725 exercises) covering past tenses and comparatives, everyday communicative vocabulary, and graded reading passages up to 200 words.

B1 · Threshold

Intermediate Level (B1)

What the learner can do: Understand the main points of clear standard input on familiar matters regularly encountered in work, school, and leisure. Deal with most situations likely to arise whilst travelling in an English-speaking area. Produce simple connected text on topics of personal interest.

FIEZEL B1 Content: 52 grammar lessons (1,300 exercises) covering conditionals and passive voice, workplace vocabulary, and informational reading passages.

B2 · Vantage

Upper-Intermediate Level (B2)

What the learner can do: Understand the main ideas of complex text on both concrete and abstract topics, including technical discussions in their field of specialization. Interact with a degree of fluency and spontaneity that makes regular interaction with native speakers quite possible without strain for either party.

FIEZEL B2 Content: 39 grammar lessons (975 exercises), nuanced vocabulary collocations, extended multi-speaker listening discussions, and analytical reading articles.

C1 · Effective Operational

Advanced Level (C1)

What the learner can do: Understand a wide range of demanding, longer texts, and recognize implicit meaning. Express ideas fluently and spontaneously without much obvious searching for expressions. Use language flexibly and effectively for social, academic, and professional purposes.

FIEZEL C1 Content: 24 grammar lessons (600 exercises) covering grammatical inversions and cleft sentences, academic reading texts, and complex discourse exercises.

C2 · Mastery

Mastery Level (C2)

What the learner can do: Understand with ease virtually everything heard or read. Summarize information from different spoken and written sources, reconstructing arguments and accounts in a coherent presentation. Express themselves spontaneously, very fluently, and precisely, differentiating finer shades of meaning even in more complex situations.

FIEZEL C2 Content: 19 grammar lessons (475 exercises) focusing on stylistic nuance, advanced idioms, critical reading passages, and subtle rhetorical differentiation.
