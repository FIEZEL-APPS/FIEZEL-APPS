FIEZEL Grammar Practice — English Grammar by CEFR Level

Does FIEZEL teach English grammar?

Yes. FIEZEL contains 180 comprehensive English grammar lessons mapped across CEFR levels A1, A2, B1, B2, C1, and C2. Each lesson includes exactly 25 focused interactive exercises (4,500 total questions). Every question provides natural feedback explaining why incorrect options fail, and mistakes return in subsequent reviews via adaptive repetition.

Grammar learning in FIEZEL is organized around actionable sentence construction rather than rote rule memorization. Learners explore core tenses, modal auxiliary systems, passive constructions, relative clauses, and complex discourse structures through active retrieval practice.

Grammar Curriculum Structure (A1 to C2)

The 180 grammar lessons are distributed systematically across the six CEFR proficiency bands:

CEFR Level Lessons Total Exercises Key Grammatical Concepts Covered
A1 (Breakthrough) 17 lessons 425 exercises Present simple, to be, subject pronouns, possessive adjectives, basic articles, singular/plural nouns, simple prepositions of place and time.
A2 (Waystage) 29 lessons 725 exercises Past simple regular & irregular verbs, past continuous, present continuous for future, comparative and superlative adjectives, basic modal verbs (can, must, should).
B1 (Threshold) 52 lessons 1,300 exercises Present perfect simple & continuous, first and second conditionals, passive voice introduction, relative clauses, modal verbs of deduction, gerunds vs. infinitives.
B2 (Vantage) 39 lessons 975 exercises Past perfect continuous, third and mixed conditionals, passive reporting verbs, complex wish/regret structures, future perfect, advanced linking adverbials.
C1 (Effective Operational) 24 lessons 600 exercises Negative and restrictive inversions, cleft sentences (wh- and it-clefts), subjunctive constructions, participle clauses, subtle modal nuance.
C2 (Mastery) 19 lessons 475 exercises Advanced discourse markers, rhetorical fronting, register and style calibration, idiomatic syntactic patterns, complex ellipsis and substitution.

The 25-Question Focused Lesson Contract

Unlike generic multiple-choice quizzes, every grammar lesson in FIEZEL follows an engineered pedagogical blueprint:

  • Form, Meaning & Function: Exercises test grammatical forms across multiple communicative contexts rather than repeating a single phrase.
  • Misconception-Specific Distractors: Each incorrect answer choice (distractor) represents a well-documented learner misconception. When a student selects a distractor, FIEZEL explains specifically why that choice is grammatically incorrect.
  • Adaptive Remediation: If a student struggles with a specific grammatical rule, Braincore flags the underlying subskill and schedules focused review in subsequent study sessions.
  • Zero Clutter: Clean, distraction-free interface optimized for fast mobile or desktop practice.
